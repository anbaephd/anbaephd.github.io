What is the SDU SE - BSc in Engineering - TEK Bachelorproject?

  1. "Løsning af en teoretisk, eksperimentelt eller en praktisk problemstilling."
  2. "En bachelorprojektrapport, der afleveres til bedømmelse og indgår i bachelorprojekteksamen."
  3. "En bachelorprojekteksamen"

Reference: Bachelorprojekt består af følgende elementer

Your Steps

  1. TEK Bachelorprojektkontrakt
    • Template: TEK Bachelorprojektkontrakt – Arbejdsdokument
    • Project description including a Gantt chart, the problem statement, research questions, and references
  2. Work on your project
    • The most important part of this project is the report. See One approach to create a report.
  3. The examination
    • Review the structure of the Examination Format.

Deadlines

  1. By 31/01/2025, upload your TEK Bachelorprojektkontrakt to spoc.sdu.dk/form/1216
  2. The project period starts on February 1, 2025, or upon approval of your TEK Bachelorprojektkontrakt
  3. By 02/06/2025, upload your Bachelorprojektrapport to digitaleksamen.sdu.dk
  4. The examination will be held in June 2025
