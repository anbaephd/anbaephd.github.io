About Me

I am a PhD Fellow in Software Engineering at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU). My research studies how to make machine learning systems reliable in production, from a software engineering and software architecture point of view. I focus on operational metrics, self-adaptive and self-managing architectures, and scalable AI pipelines.

I bring industry experience as an ML engineer at Energinet and a data scientist at Esoft. I hold an MSc in Mathematical Modeling and Computation from DTU and a BSc in Robot Systems from SDU. During my doctoral studies, I have been a teaching assistant for the Big Data and Data Science Technologies course and have supervised more than 50 BSc and BEng students on their final projects.
